What is Football?

Football (also called soccer) is a team sport played between two teams of 11 players. The goal is simple: score more goals than the other team by getting the ball into their net. It is played in almost every country and needs very little equipment, just a ball and some space.

  • 22 players on the pitch
  • 90 minutes per match
  • 250M+ players worldwide

A Short History

Games of kicking a ball have existed for thousands of years in places like ancient China and Greece. Modern football began in England in 1863, when the first official rules were written down. Since then, the sport has spread to every continent and become a huge part of many cultures.

How to Play

  1. Each team has 11 players, including one goalkeeper.

  2. The match starts with a kick-off from the center of the pitch.

  3. Players pass, dribble, and shoot using their feet, body, and head.

  4. Only the goalkeeper can use their hands, and only inside their own box.

  5. A goal counts when the whole ball crosses the goal line.

  6. The team with the most goals after 90 minutes wins.

Player Positions

Each position has a role, work together to score a goal

Goalkeeper

Stops shots and protects the goal.

Defenders

Stop the other team from attacking.

Midfielders

Connect defense and attack, and control the game.

Forwards

Their main job is to score goals.

Basic Rules

  • Fouls: Pushing, tripping, or holding gives the other team a free kick.
  • Handball: Touching the ball with hands or arms (except the goalkeeper) is a foul.
  • Offside: You can't be ahead of the last defender when the ball is passed to you.
  • Throw-in: When the ball goes out on the side, it's thrown back in with both hands.
  • Yellow card: A warning.
  • Red card: The player is sent off the pitch.
